About

Celebrations, lit with intent

Shrikara SFX is an Indore-based special effects team for weddings and events. We bring cold pyro, sparkulars, CO₂, confetti, smoke and synchronised fireworks to celebrations across Madhya Pradesh and nearby regions.

Every effect is planned around a moment that matters to you: the entry, the varmala, the cake or the countdown. When it goes off, your guests feel it as well as see it.
